The Scaling Crisis
Why first-round interviews fail at scale
  • A team of 10 cannot meaningfully interview 500+ applicants per role
  • Interview quality degrades under fatigue, time pressure, and volume
  • No standardised evidence trail — hiring decisions are disputed
The Research Question
Can AI replicate a skilled interviewer?

Can an AI conduct a first-round interview that is simultaneously more consistent, more objective, more scalable, and more conversationally natural than a human interviewer?

Four Core Structural Problems
Traditional recruitment failures
  • Interviewer Bias — Different interviewers apply different unconscious rubrics, making cross-candidate comparison legally exposed
  • Scaling Bottleneck — Human-led interviews cannot scale linearly with application volume
  • Experience Disparity — Candidates on different days or with different interviewers get unequal experiences
  • No Objective Scoring — Holistic impressions cannot be audited, appealed, or compared programmatically
02

Problem Statement

How can an AI system autonomously conduct structured technical interviews in real time, dynamically adapting its questioning strategy based on candidate responses, objectively evaluating skill depth through evidence extraction, and producing auditable, comparative candidate rankings — all while delivering a conversational experience indistinguishable from a human-led interview?

Decomposed into 5 Sub-Problems

P1 — Real-Time Multimodal Communication

How to deliver an avatar-driven interview with sub-second STT and TTS latency over WebRTC without breaks in conversational flow.

P2 — Adaptive Dialogue Management

How to route candidate utterances to the correct AI pathway (fast conversation vs. deep background analysis) without perceptible delay.

P3 — Objective Skill Assessment

How to assess the depth of competency, not just keyword presence. Saying "Redis" vs. architecting a Redis Cluster are not equivalent.

P4 — Cross-Time Candidate Ranking

How to fairly compare candidates interviewed weeks apart on a consistent, mathematically grounded scale.

P5 — Interview Integrity Assurance

How to detect behaviours indicating external assistance (multiple faces, phone, tab switching, AI-coached responses) in real time without disrupting the interview.

Literature Review

Existing System 1
Async Video Interview Platforms

HireVue · Spark Hire · VidCruiter

  • Candidates record video answers to fixed questions
  • AI scores on speech patterns and historical hiring data

Limitation:

Not interactive. No follow-up. Models perpetuate demographic biases from historical data [1].

Existing System 2
Conversational Chatbot Screeners

Mya (Paradox/Olivia) · Jobpal

  • Text-based NLP screening
  • Filter by keyword matching

Limitation:

Rule-based. Evaluates keyword existence, not depth. Cannot probe or adapt.

Existing System 3
LLM-Based Interview Agents

Interviewer.AI · GPT-4 prototypes

  • Fully conversational, adaptive follow-up questions
  • Adapts based on candidate responses

Limitation:

Holistic/narrative evaluation only. No evidence-backed skill depth score. High latency on every turn.

Existing Framework
Structured Interview Frameworks (STAR)

Campion et al. (1997) [2]

  • Structured interviews have higher predictive validity
  • STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result

Limitation:

Requires human evaluators. Binary (good/bad), not multi-level depth.

Related Theory
Elo Rating System — Elo (1978) [3]
  • Pairwise comparisons produce stable predictive rankings
  • Used in chess, competitive programming, benchmarks
  • No simultaneous head-to-head competition required

Research Objectives

Primary Objective: Design and implement a fully automated, real-time AI interview system for any role defined by a JD, producing objective, evidence-backed skill assessments and comparative candidate rankings.

O1
Hybrid Routing Architecture
Deliver conversational responses within <300ms routing time while asynchronous deep-analysis tasks run in parallel.
O2
JD-Driven Skill Ladder Generation
Translate unstructured JD text into per-skill 5-level binary assessment rubrics using an LLM.
O3
Real-Time Evidence Extraction
Evaluate Q&A exchanges against skill ladder binary questions, extracting verbatim transcript citations as auditable evidence.
O4
Dynamic Skill Tree
Maintain a growing candidate skill tree throughout the interview for intelligent question selection targeting unexplored depths.
O5
Elo-Based Candidate Ranking
Enable fair, time-independent comparison of candidates for the same role by converting skill scores to Elo pairwise match outcomes.
O6
Real-Time Profile Verification
Cross-reference candidate verbal claims with GitHub, LinkedIn, and resume data to produce a quantified trust score.
O7
Anti-Cheating Proctoring
Detect and flag 10 behavioural anomaly types (faces, phone, tab switch, AI assistance) in real time without disrupting flow.

Score + Elo Algorithm
// Weighted Score
score(s) = level(s) × weight(s)
total = Σ score(s_i)
pct = (total / 5.0) × 100

// Elo (K=32, default=1500)
ΔR = 32 × (actual − expected)
expected = 1 / (1 + 10^((Rb−Ra)/400))
